I was greeted by blue, green, yellow, and red confetti when opening the laundry door. Of all the doors, this was the last one I’d hope to find a surprise behind. But the damage was done. The impact was slightly cushioned by the fact that d makes sure to flip his clothes inside out before throwing them in the wash.

Prying it open carefully, the [tattered pattern](https://oliviailla.blog/2026/10/24/thought.html) read *Como ye ho*, with none of the original imagery visible. It turned out to be a T-shirt given to d by his host family in Asturias 15 years ago, after noticing his talent for solving Rubik's cubes. 

*Alright, that's a sweet memory.* Still annoyed by the mess.

I scooped up the sprinkles with the damp T-shirt, the final purpose the piece served before being chucked in the bin. As much as d is good at recycling the same clothes until they crumble and fray, some are better off archived back at home, much like most of my own wardrobe, shamefully.
