## STEP-BY-STEP ON LEAVING YOUR PHONE/ THE APPS:

1. Get annoyed and resentful at tech.

    *I am the problem! It has taken over my life!*

2. Detox or leave social media.

    *Fueling dopamine hits impairs our sense of gratification.*

3. Change your circumstances.

    *Cut off stressful people, news, and projects.*

4. Live soberly.

    *Get comfortable with boredom. "What’s my purpose in life?"*

    *"The crowd is your king", as [Taylor](https://www.reddit.com/r/TaylorSwift/comments/1o0rcy6/the_crowd_is_your_king_the_mousetrap_in_the_hamlet/) says in her promotion. The internet is a mirror ball.*

5. Adopt a ~~boring~~ passion.

    *Running is the ultimate lonely sport,*

    *Or read if you have trouble articulating emotions and thoughts.*

6. Lose ~~online~~ friends with a contrarian thought.

    *I’ve done it over a bowl of ramen in March. "We don’t talk anymore."*

7. Go your own way.

    *People panic when you leave.*

8. Be alone on the global internet.

    *Is **this** it? A sanctuary where only I exist?*

9. Embrace your new freedom!

    *Relapse? Watch a full show or movie without your phone and write about it.*
