## This scene reminds me of

After their meet-cute at the hospital (aside from the crash itself), Almut is having her smoothie and asks Tobias, in neck brace, what he does for a living. He answers, "Weetabix sales" and she casually responds being a chef and preparing to open a anglo-bavarian restaurant.

This introductory exchange is similar to the final scene in The Drama (2026), where Charlie sits with a beaten-up face while Emma (re)introduces herself rather than accept the apology. Both female leads play the chill and calm partner.
