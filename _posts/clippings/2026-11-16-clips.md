> The "Dilbert principle" is developed by Scott Adams which states that companies systematically promote incompetent employees to management positions in order to get them out of the workflow. According to the principle, the majority of real, productive work in a company is done by people lower in the power ladder.

Two tectonic plates shifting forcefully over the events of the past three years. My work dynamic makes so much sense now!

But I also hate to admit it, lately I’ve finally managed to lug half a leg up that ladder, only to get dragged right back down whenever a scapegoat is needed to make an example for the newcomers, or when they realize I’m getting too comfy. 

Can’t believe I still tolerate their behavior…
