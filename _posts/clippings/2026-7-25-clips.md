> In order to explore, to be open to new ideas and experiences—all core to eroticism, in Perel’s view—people have to feel stable enough to let their guard down. 

> They have to unclench.

> The weight of the world—the dismal news alerts, the rent bills, the doomscrolling—isn’t putting them in the mood for lighthearted banter or seductive dancing or organic inquisitiveness.

> [R]egardless of relationship status, is essentially this: Never give yourself over to numbness.


To feel is to be human.
