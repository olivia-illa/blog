> The word תִּמְשָׁל is a second person future imperative of “to govern”, so it quite literally is an order “you will govern”. It doesn’t translate as “do thou,” “thou shalt,” or “thou mayest.”  

Timshel: thou mayest.

"Thou mayest rule over sin." 

> Steinbeck claims that this is important because, since Cain slew Abel, we are all descended from Cain. God’s promise is then that we as humans have *a choice*. If we choose, we may rule over sin.
