### Immediate Review

★★★☆☆

I'll have to sit with the ending a bit longer for fair review, but here's my immediate reaction to it:

Olivia's acting throws me off. I like that her films are always stunningly shot--the structural lines and interior framing creating a scene within a scene, and the harmonious color palettes paired with lighting and music that pull the characters' emotions together.

Yet ultimately the dialogues and plot just fell flat for me. I've watched *Don't Worry Darling (2022*) too, which falls short for that reason too. Actually worse; the plot made no sense I remember. This latest one is one that aims to capture the subtle dynamics and intricacies of love, but fails to strike a cord with me, unlike The Drama (2026) does. 

I know I'm not pinpointing what's bad about, it maybe it is because there's not enough substance for me to pull from... I'll deliver something more concrete when I think of it.
