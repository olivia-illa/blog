If you ask me why I don’t have Instagram on my phone, or the YouTube app, and insist on accessing them from the browser [¹](#fn1), here’s why:

**I refuse to let HOURS of my life be stolen from me! It’s no longer a minute here and there, but entire hours when I get on.**

I was just caught red-handed by me self! After finding out I still had five hours until the release of the PATIENT ZER() tracks, I decided to scroll around a bit, you know, catch up on the (maylor) lore and see what everyone else was anticipating from the new clip.

An hour later, I found myself doom-scrolling in a doggy-nap position, neck cranked, eyes sore, having learned absolutely nothing except a few more *engagements*.

<div class="botanical-divider">・・・・・</div>

These short-form video apps are the most maliciously addictive devices, and I need a physical barrier—a technical friction, if you will—to stop myself. My fingers still unconsciously search for them even after removing it for weeks [²](#fn2). It’s a terminal illness for anyone without self-awareness.

The feeling of an hour just slipping through my fingertips upsets then frustrates me (hi, hence why I’m documenting this). Yet deleting my digital presence entirely feels a bit too extreme, so this browser--only setup has been my new coping mechanism, except when Tay announces a drop. I do love that she never makes us wait more than a month these days.
