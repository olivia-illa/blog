*The M&Ms* were requested by our legal, and so were the *Kirkland Chocolate Almonds*.

I was shocked to discover that other adults were also into nibbling on chocolate during work.

*"Not all heroes wear capes—some just bring chocolate."*

I'm pretty sure the sugar in those only fogs the mind for most, but not for me. I thrive on chocolate. It runs in my veins.

<br>

**m:** "Don't you think they taste bitter? Not like the ones you used to order."

**Me:** "I'm pretty sure that's just the almond skin."

When m joined the team, we bonded over our shared love for chocolate—a simple happiness adults seemingly grow out of once they discover bitter and better drugs like coffee. The only thing that separates us is his preference for milk over dark, which I'd sort out whenever I grabbed a handful of *Swiss Delice*🍫 from the cabinet.
