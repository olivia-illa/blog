It's sipping a latte (with fresh milk froth added) on a Saturday morning and immersed in the birds' **TWEEDLE-TSEEE**, the fat white cat's sudden **MEEE-OWWW** from downstairs (for breakfast), the **RUMBLE...RUMBLE...** of a heavy loaded truck and the **THWIP-TAP-TAP** of my own thoughts fed to my blog rather than my bot.

I am content with my hot coffee.
