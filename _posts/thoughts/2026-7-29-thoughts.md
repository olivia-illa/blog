Someone should invent an app that shuts down work laptops for 15min after an hour of continuous output[¹](#fn1), and turns them off completely after six hours of total daily use[²](#fn2). Forcing an 8hr schedule on employees who have no meetings and are evaluated purely on task completion feels like a 9hr prison sentence of play-pretend. You can only stare at a screen for so long before the eyes dry out and the mind boggles and hallucinates with destructive, rather than constructive, thoughts. Its not the assigned work tasks we're burnt out from, but the 9hr obligation that companies (or managers), especially in (east) Asia, still believe is the only viable measurement of devotion. 

How about measuring how well one can manage being productive at work while maintaining a healthy lifestyle as proof of sustainability.
