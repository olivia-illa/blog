### Welcome home 🎉

When d finally came back, the bottom of his suitcase was sprinkled with what can only be described as cockroach poop from Amsterdam. But what was sweeter was the crinkly Brottüte d's mom had crammed in there, which to my delight was filled with its own wonders:

- two white undershirts with tags cut[¹](#fn1)
- two boxes of Italian biscuits with sticky notes: "Für o's parents" and "Für o's grandma"
- a mini pack of chocolate coated *Leibniz*
- a taped up *Alete Schnitties*[²](#fn2) box that I thought was either half eaten or bursted by accident; turned out was stuffed with chocolate bars, single-pack coffee treats and ofc Schnitties
