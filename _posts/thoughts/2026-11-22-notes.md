My blog landed on the first page of my Google search. Biased? 

Perhaps so, since I do look up my own page a bit 🤷🏻‍♀️ I was re-establishing citation links I’d missed and saw it come up. Then t, ever enthusiastic about any math related implementation, and d introduced me to the concept of **PageRank**, which works by

> counting the number and quality of links to a page to determine a rough estimate of how important the website is. The underlying assumption is that more important websites are likely to receive more links from other websites.

— from [Google via Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PageRank)

My page has way more outbound links than inbound ones. Imagine a day when random netizens would share my posts. My takes are *that* original, I’m sure of it…
