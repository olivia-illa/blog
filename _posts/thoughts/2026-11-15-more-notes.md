Let’s try naming the seven sins before we start the movie.

“Killing, stealing… lying?”

They’re more in the biblical sense, like character flaws and morals.

### Seven deadly sins in the context of [Dante's *Divine Comedy*](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskScienceFiction/comments/4mld0u/the_divine_comedy_why_are_there_nine_circles_of/):

1. Pride — *superbia*
2. Greed — *avaritia*
3. Envy — *invidia*
4. Wrath — *ira*
5. Lust — *luxuria*
6. Gluttony — *gula*
7. Sloth — *acedia*
