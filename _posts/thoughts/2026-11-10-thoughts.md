Yes, we *did* just pre-celebrate Dad's birthday with [surf and turf](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CZHN5eRddwn7de8p6). 

And yes, the [throbbing pain](https://oliviailla.blog/2026/11/08/sore-ankle.html) in my right lateral ligament (ankle) instantly got worse as I carved down the plate of buttered corn, medium-rare ribeye, perfectly cooked Boston lobster, roasted Brussels sprouts, and creamed spinach. How could I forget about the unlimited thin fries, too?

*What are the bloody chances, right?*

Right as Dad was giving his morbid speech about the grandfather I never met--warning us that dying young is in my genes and that d and I should chase our dreams early—-I immediately started experiencing the exact same pain Dad gets from living a life *too abundant.*

*Perfect timing.*

I can't even remember the last time I had shellfish besides shrimp! I have low iron, for God's sake!


<br>

**P.S.** The meal (for four) cost me a *foot*, that's for sure. And no one's *footing* it for me. 

*Sorry I couldn't help it.*

**Edits.** This is not funny. I've been chugging water. It feels like as if my neck muscles have gone limp and any movement to my heavy head would hurt. Now apply that feeling to my right foot and ankle. Even lifting in mid air hurt, so I drag my foot slowly like in ballet, drawing circles with my toes, except in acute pain and a lot less gracefully.
