d handles the cooking now and then, often more than willingly, which I believe tickles the creative side of his brain. 

Tonight he decided to make a fusion cream shrimp linguine by adding green peppers. He’d picked up two types from the market: shishito peppers and green chilies. They’re similar in size and color, except one has a wrinkly texture and a completely different flavor profile. The former is sweet with a subtle smokiness when sautéed—one of my favorite vegetables to throw into stir-fries—whereas the other is purely a hot pepper, meant to be deseeded for sharp heat.

d called out from the kitchen while I was busy sloperating two pages a day:

**d:** We’re f-ed. The green chilies are actually spicy.

**Me:** What do you mean? Aren’t they just Korean chili peppers?

**d:** Well, they’re not, and I cut both of them into the exact same size.

I sampled a piece, figuring d was exaggerating, even though he handles spice far better than I do. Instantly my ears began to burn, followed by my stomach—a grenade had detonated, sending the remainder of the gunpowder straight up to my ears as the burn spread across my tongue and gums where I’d last chewed. 

*Guess we’re playing Russian roulette tonight.*
