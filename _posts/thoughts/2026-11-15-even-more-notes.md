Correction tapes must not be common where d grew up, cuz he managed to break one, then fixed it his way.

Anyone who joins d’s company is expected to take apart a personal machine of their choice, put it back together, and demonstrate that it still works. d chose his decade old electric razor, which he finally replaced with a new one last year.

The other day, I suggested that d journal to vent his frustrations, even though I knew he wasn’t big on pen and paper. But for the sake of my own future, I was going to make him try. Halfway through, he asked for a whiteout, and I handed him the [one](https://www.pentel.co.uk/product/plus-correction-tape-mr/?srsltid=AYCX9vJxwBEnDzc_Jcx1iksJcbx9Lgk96Vy0inY-0xRWzPATCSKsvf25) I’ve had since middle school.

He proceeded to roll it on the blank side, snapping the tape on the take-up gear. I figured, like fixing a cassette tape, he’d simply wind it back. Instead, he grabbed his tool kit, and cracked open the entire casing.

**Me:** What are you doing? Why are tweezers involved?

**d:** I’m trying to unravel the used tape gear, cut off the damaged section, and tape the ends back together.

**Me:** What…you broke it?

**d:** I’m using the remaining white part, the sticky bit (see),to splice the ends back together.

*I watched as he used the tweezers to slowly thread the tape back onto its gears.*

**d:** Look, it’s all good now.

Now and then, I remind myself to put a little more faith in that man. Give him the time he needs to sort things out, his way.
