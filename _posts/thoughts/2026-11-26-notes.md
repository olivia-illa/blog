I forgot to wear the one thing I needed for training: running shoes.

**d:** Can't you run in those? You spent a fortune on them.

**Me:** They're my unnecessarily-expensive-walking-shoes. Plus I've had them for years.

*(Motioning the On Cloud X4s.)*


d was supposed to accompany me on my run (with less than a month of training left) but I ended up tagging along him while he free-skated along the riverside. From behind, he looked like a chicken on wheels, his limbs pushing out in alternating, opposite directions. He kept his eyes locked straight ahead to engage his core and watch out for bumps, swaying his hips side to side to build momentum.

I tried it once, gripping the railings, and my legs kept rolling apart in opposite directions. I immediately felt the burn in my inner thighs. How he does it hands-free over long distances amazes me.
