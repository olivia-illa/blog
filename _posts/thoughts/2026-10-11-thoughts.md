Tyler from *Remember Me (2010)* reminds me of a particular lost young man. He comes from a beautiful, almost picture perfect family, maintains a close relationship with his older brother, appears scuffed from all his vices and lonely thoughts. All he wanted then, was to be taken seriously, and loved by the ones he care. And I felt for him.

He also called her Ally.

> Whatever you do in life will be insignificant, but it's very important that you do it. Because nobody else will. 


I came into this with no hint of the setting or plot, but wanting to hear young Robert Pattinson's voice.
