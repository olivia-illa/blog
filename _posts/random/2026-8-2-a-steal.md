It's swiping through BookTok and instantly being influenced to buy the latest trending memoir, despite having hundreds of books already on my list that are far more relevant to my current state of mind. It’s a total steal of my mental peace, because now I *must* possess it. The title immediately takes over my thoughts, my feed's algorithm, and my search history.

### Tactic #1: Secondhand Bookshelves  
I start by scouring the web for the best deals. If time isn’t an issue, waiting for a generous avid reader to donate a copy to a secondhand shop can save me up to 70%, depending on condition.

### Tactic #2: Monthly Coupons  
My second move is collecting coupons and timing purchases with monthly bookstore promos. Ordering foreign editions online usually brings a 20–25% discount, plus the additional $3 saved using cash vouchers[¹](#fn1).

### Tactic #3: New Memberships  
The real icing on the cake is signing up with a new account to trigger the newbie welcome coupon, shaving off another $3.

### Tactic #4: Gift Cards & Points  
Stacking credit card cash-back rebates or company holiday gift cards brings the final total to zero, making the book completely free (by girl math logic, at least)!

### Tactic #5: Muling via Friends  
Alternatively, I could ask a friend or relative to grab a copy from Barnes & Noble or Amazon while abroad[²](#fn2). In-store deals can be great (sometimes), especially since where I’m at, foreign books imported locally carry a steep markup. But between soaring FX rates and wasting precious suitcase weight, it's rarely worth the hassle.

And that is how, a month and a half after the initial impulse (right as I dragged myself through the final chapters of [Yesteryear](https://oliviailla.blog/2026/08/01/Yesteryear.html)) *Strangers: A Memoir of Marriage* arrived the very next day. A sign that it was time to move on.


<br>

I scored the hardcover for $19 total (including overseas shipping and exchange rates), which I’d like to believe the bookstore ordered just for me hence the wait time. 

An absolute steal!
