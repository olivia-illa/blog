For now, I would describe that day in fragments. I remembered being so relaxed and at ease, it felt like a hazy dream or at a [*wellness session*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q99KYhD9BpQ).

- 14:30, after a 1.5hr session and quick lunch
- 27 degrees outside with a gentle breeze
- A pair of sneakers and athleisure wear
- Two bikes stuffed with gummies and two full water bottles
- A tranquil green strip by the riverside, 12k away
- A beach blanket and a race towel
- A tall tree with petal foliage against peeks of the blue heaven
- [*Careless People*](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/223436601-careless-people) (2026) and [*Bröder*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/251062317-broder) (2026)
- A pair of smiley almond eyes squinting at me
- Calbee fries, one rice cracker, and airplane Cheez-Its
- A boomerang dug straight into earth multiple times
- A gust gave the *silicone smiley watermelon* a playful lift
- A cyclist dodged the plastic disk that flew his way
- A son and father playing soft ball
- A poodle on its hind legs
- 17:20, pinkish coral painted over baby blue hues
- A quick stop for ingredients
- Two bowls of stir-fried udon with veggies and Netflix


A perfect day is so simple when things are right.
