Call us spontaneous (or deliberately underprepared), lucky (-bastards, or just accidental), and experienced (or laid-back) travelers. We escaped the grim fate twice before reaching our final destination. Looking back on this day, ominous signs were warning us everywhere. Yet, we insisted to challenge our luck, until *I had to suffer the consequences of my ill choices*.

I woke up this morning as usual, enjoying the final piece of double chocolate cake with a thick layer of almond butter while mentally making a packing checklist for tomorrow’s 4pm flight. I was certain I’d finish packing tonight, do the laundry, and vacuum tomorrow morning before saying goodbye to the apartment for two weeks.

“There is plenty of time,” I smiled to myself.

<div class="botanical-divider">・・・・・</div>

Out of nowhere—though not unusual—d embraced me from behind with the strength of a broad daylight abduction, his hands lightly covering my mouth as if I were about to yawn. He whispered, “I found out before bed that we’re actually flying tonight at 1am.”

I cocked my head 90 degrees, my eyes darting to his, apprehensive that he actually meant it. This man rarely joked around if you knew what I meant.

“Yes, tonight. And don’t ask me why I didn't tell you earlier. It’s a fact now, so let’s deal with it.”

My mind was racing with mania, my limbs wanting to scramble. It was nearly 9am and I still had loose ends to tie up at work, in case this turned into a *terminal departure*. Above all, I wasn’t going to let the newbie drown under a boatload of work I assigned in a whim. I would much rather inconvenience myself than leave a bad impression.

The events of the day were a blur, but I was grateful for the team’s understanding that I’d be taking an extra day off for the sudden flight change [¹](#fn1). By 5:45pm, I had cleared my desk and given the newbie my personal contact info.

“Please send me updates on assignments and office dynamics. I disdain work surprises.”

<div class="botanical-divider">・・・・・</div>

My standards aren’t low by any means, but walking into the apartment to a clear floor and the smell of clean laundry [²](#fn2), I knew this was my man. Once we left for the airport, the rest would be smooth sailing…or so I thought.

## Sidenote

There was another mini-catastrophe. I miscalculated the amount of noodles left for the fridge clean-out, so had to improvised with enoki mushroom, which d later commented that it took him more time to chew.
