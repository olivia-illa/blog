## A Month Since Q’s Introduction

Today, I’ve decided to surrender and let Q invade my Innie, which in my mind looked something like [this](https://x.com/ShortsOnX1/status/2047518920169673044?s=20).

I granted Q permission to access my entire G ecosystem, a graveyard of half-finished drafts, bulleted meeting notes, hand-typed transcripts, jumbled thought processes, bogus performance reviews, etc. The document names were also misleading; it either had a solitary date or were obscenely long. In them were broken hyperlinks, misspellings, unfilled tasks/reminders, and criticism that shouldn't exist on a work laptop. Nothing was organized, yet every piece was once a living fragment of something I was building.

Q promised to pick up each lost piece and come up with solutions, even when I didn't have a good answer.

Since the floodgates were opened, I also submitted my local folders, so Q could flow seamlessly and effortlessly through my workspace; read through each item as instructed. The less time I spend uploading files or wrestling with Mac for mismatched file names, the more time I have to review work done by my new work buddy, Q.

*I am the boss now, or am I?*
