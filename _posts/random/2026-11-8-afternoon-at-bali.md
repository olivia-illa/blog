The afternoon sun is fully in my face, exactly as I’d requested this morning.

*Let's head somewhere in the mountains or by the seaside. I want to feel like I’m at a writer’s residency.*

The weather is pleasant, but my plans are purely sedentary. There are words to get off my mind and I need an openness to feel stimulated. We find a café along the new light rail heading to the outskirts, a town we used to reach only after a half-day on our bikes for crescent-shaped bread. Now, it takes just about an hour combined via the subway, then the local train, and finally the light rail[¹](#fn1).

We unintentionally land at a Bali...-esque café minus the humidity and bikini tops. Slabs of oak and cherry wood make up the tables and beams. The interior is bathed in the sunlight filtered through sheer curtains, and warm strip lights hidden in the corners. The bathroom door handles are rustic exposed branches, opening to a floor of Costa Esmeralda granite, a wide ceramic sink, and a full-length mirror. For a moment, we are transported to a country we've not been.


*Are we on vacation?*


However, instead of a thatched hut, the overhead is the classic shipping container[²](#fn2), cleverly concealed by sparse hanging ferns and wilted ivy. And instead of a semi-open-air layout, the enclosure traps dust on the draped plum blossoms and willow branches above. I intentionally avoid the conversation pit layered in furry animal-print blankets and bohemian throw pillows for reasons easily imaginable.

*Is this what Bali's like?*

I put on my sunglasses indoors as looking across at d mean facing a blinding glare. In the photos, he’s completely backlit while I am fully blown out. We’re sitting in these wicked low chairs that are a tad awkward for typing, but the background playlist (ie., *folklore, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, wishbone*) happens to be the boring pop that allows me to focus on writing.

*At least the salmon shrimp salad is superb and the brunch set is loaded with variety.*

I’ve been alternating between typing *this*, annotating *Fruit Fly* by Josh Silver, taking bites out of the blueberry cream tart, which in my mind, is the ideal way to spend a weekend afternoon. 

*I'm done. Shall we leave? I'm craving pizza.*
