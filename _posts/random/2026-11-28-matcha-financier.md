The ingredients are classic French staples: butter, sugar, egg whites, and almond flour(?). So I figured something was off with my technique. I stumbled onto a dubbed [Korean pastry chef](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KTugGqBAO8) breaking down the science behind this finicky dessert on a whiteboard.

## Techniques I learnt

### Browned Butter (solids)
- Simmer over **low heat** to trigger the Maillard reaction.
- Stir and scrape the bottom of the pan so nothing burns.
- Look for nutty, butterscotch, hazelnut aromas and color.

### Emulsification (same temp)
- Let egg whites come to room temperature.
- Cool the browned butter down to room temperature (below 50°C).
- If emulsification fails, butter seeps out during baking.
- Stir the egg whites gently to not whip in too much air.
- Whisk the matcha powder into the browned butter until smooth (this is harder it seems).

### Glossy Batter
- Sift dry ingredients ahead of time to avoid lumps.
- Fold in the flour last and mix thoroughly.
- Overmixing can create a [hollow bottom](https://youtu.be/kBZCUUZbk-g?si=_L3uv2iY_a5hdMxs&t=315).
- Chill the batter in the fridge for at least 3hr
- Lift and fold the bottom of batter before filling up the pipping bag

## Ingredients for Matcha Financiers

For 7 financiers cuz I fill each cavity to the brim 🤷🏻‍♀️

### 🧈 Brown the Butter and Cool to Room Temp
- 66 g unsalted butter
- 3 g matcha powder (ensure no lumps)

### 🧈 Mix the Wet Ingredients in a Bowl
- 63 g egg whites (2 eggs)
- 59 g granulated sugar
- 0.5 g salt
- 3.5 g honey
- 1.5 g vanilla bean paste

### 🧈 Sift and Fold into the Wet
- 33 g almond flour
- 28 g all-purpose flour
