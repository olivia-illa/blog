Knowing I'll mess up a sponge cake, despite my success a few months ago for c's bday, I skipped the crucial step in the [Hokkaido Double Fromage Cheesecake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lNQ3i9pNxU4), and replaced it with a classic crust for d’s big 30ᵗʰ.


I suggest baking the following measurement in a slightly smaller spring form then an 18cm, as mine came out shorter than I'd preferred.

<br>

### Classic Crust Layer

## 🧀 Pack down the wet sand

- ⅓ pack of wholewheat Leibniz (pantry biscuits)
- a corner of butter + drizzles of olive oil
- pinch of salt + 1 tsp sugar

Bake for 15min at 170'C

<br>

### Baked Cheesecake Layer

## 🧀 Melt white chocolate
- 80g heated heavy cream
- 60g chopped white chocolate

## 🧀 Cream Together in Room Temp with the Above
- 100g cream cheese
- 90g mascarpone
- 20g white sugar
- 1 egg
- Vanilla
- 10g AP flour (optional, cuz I forgot mine[¹](#fn1))

Bake for 35min at 170'C in bath water

<br>

### Chilled Mascarpone Layer

## 🧀 Melt Dark Chocolate
- 100g heated heavy cream
- 50g chopped dark chocolate

## 🧀 Soak gelatin sheet in cold water
- 2.5g gelatin sheet, wrung out
- 15g hot water

## 🧀Cream Together in Room Temp with the Above
- 110g mascarpone
- 10g white sugar

<br>


A traditional double fromage is smeared down with whipped cream and coated in blitzed sponge cake crumbs. In my version, I shaved chocolate over the top to hide the cracks after an aggressive unmolding process [²](#fn2).
