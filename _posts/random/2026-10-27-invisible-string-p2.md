It had just begun to feel like autumn outside. The wind carried a gentle breeze, and the sunlight felt warm on my face. In a month or two, we’d be wearing light raincoats. But for now, cardigans layered perfectly over summer dresses.

With two white chocolate Bueno bars and a bag of Werther's salted caramels, t and I headed to the *[Temple of Love](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DGHzT4QNuA75VTrf7).* It was a busy day for *月老*. Adults in their early 30s piled in and out of the temple, followed by a few worried parents. I watched as a priest conducted a private rite while wandering foreigners brushed past my shoulders. Happy chaos mingled with the silent prayers of people holding love notes, the incense dust clinging to their clothes, and the curious chatter of bystanders.

One or two were dressed for the occasion in faded baby pinks and florals, while the majority blended into a sea of beige and grey sweater jackets, indifferent of gender. The women seemed slightly older, and with the same weary expressions concealed behind minimal makeup. *They’re too pretty to be single.* The men, compared to their counterparts, were lanky and lost, sporting identical hairstyles that didn't suit their head shapes.

*I hope 月老 isn't picking one of this bunch.*

t wore a chocolate-brown gingham shirt with scrunched details in the front, paired with high-waisted French jeans. A classic t look, discreet. I, on the other hand, showed up looking like a grunge *Hello Kitty*, wearing a crimson red cardigan over a heavy cotton dress in faded oxford-blue tie-dye. 

*Why not pray for a future for us too?*

Following the line of girls, t purchased a bundle of joss money and some incense from the auntie behind the counter. The auntie shoved a handful of cheap candy after she paid $15 for the service. 

*“Remember to have these afterwards.”*

I watched as t placed her offerings on a red plastic plate next to a box of dried fruits, chocolate bars, and Oreos. She began praying, moving from the stove outside to the main prayer table inside. There, she read her wishlist in silence, staring solemnly at her phone screen like everyone else in the room. Following closely behind her as I filmed, I soon found myself trapped amidst the wishful prayers. 

Instead of excusing myself like a rude outlier, I stayed and talked to Him. I shared a few near-future commitments and asked Him to subtly plant the Christmas gift I wanted this year into d's head. By the end, t had circled the premises counterclockwise, prayed to four deities, and received her red string and a piece of prayer slip that required one last wave over the stove. 

The rest was up to *月老* to decide who her man was going to be.

While t and I are opposites by definition, our preferences for men aren’t far apart, at least on the surface. So I thought it’d be fun to pluck out the ones I observe, and qualities I took for granted in d when wishing to the Genie.


### Basis

* Not married, nor has children
* Pleasant smile with healthy skin
* Good personal hygiene
* Non-smoker, non-heavy drinker *Beer doesn't count.*
* Exercises regularly
* Well-dressed *Shirts count? The bar is low for engineers.*

### Character

* Disciplined, honest, ~~punctual~~, responsible
* Committed to personal growth *Undefeated by AI? Not yet.*
* Critical thinker, an expert in his field
* Avoids excessive profanity *It’s rare when d speaks up.*
* Socially aware *Autistic relieves d of this right?*
* Politically moderate *Some things are probably off-limits.*

### Conduct

* Expresses affection and enjoys physical touch
* Listens and is non-judgmental *d tries not to laugh all the time.*
* Respects my independence
* Not envious of my success and supportive in tough times ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
* Welcoming to my family and friends
* Curious and engaged in partner's life

### Finances

* Has work-life balance *What if work = passion = money?*
* Has a stable income
* ~~Proactive~~ in legal investments
* Affords overseas trips twice a year

### Family

* Healthy family boundaries
* Not a mama's boy
* No unresolved family baggage, or heavy financial burdens
* Open to having children
* Willing to share domestic and parenting duties equally
