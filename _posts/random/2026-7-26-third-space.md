In search of accessible third spaces that meet the criteria:

* Shaded and heat-resistant (preferably with AC, but not limited to)
* Equipped with proper chairs, not stools
* Casual dress-code: I can be seen dressed down in PJ-esque tops or athleisure and not feel ashamed
* Available 24/7 or till an hour after typical dinner time
* Permits dining, snacking, and drinking ~~soft drinks only~~
* Supports a variety of ongoing activities eg., reading, board games, soft chatting
* Offers fully illuminated areas
* ~~Not a playground~~

<div class="botanical-divider">・・・・・</div>

Approved locations with ratings:

### Esoothman
* **Rating:** 9.1 / 10
* **Price:** Less than $10 for 3 hours per person
* **Hours:** Open 24/7
* **Drinks:** Unlimited drinks, fountains for coffee, tea, and soft drinks
* **Seating:** Dining chairs available only at open *soft-spoken* areas. Booths feature deep couches, not ideal for good posture, but has large windows overlooking the main street. Private rooms have desktop setups with half and full enclosures; good alternative for a night cap.
* **Atmosphere:** Extremely silent with barely-there soft piano
 

### Louisa Coffee (Banqiao)
* **Rating:** 7.5 / 10
* **Price:** No minimum spend
* **Hours:** Open till 9:00 PM (upstairs area open till 8:00 PM)
* **Drinks:** Latte grande under $4
* **Seating:** Couch seats and diner chairs line the walls, and double seaters are available in the center to form 4-seaters
* **Atmosphere:** Occasional laughter coming from high schoolers, and parenting noise



### McDonald's (Guting)
* **Rating:** 5.5 / 10
* **Price:** No minimum spend
* **Hours:** Open till 3:00 AM
* **Food:** Spent $13 for 2 burgers, 2 fries, orange juice, and an Evian bottle.
* **Seating:** 4-seaters along the walls with either dining chairs or curved benches with backs. Some tables can be separated into 2-seaters, and hungry diners will be eyeing them
* **Atmosphere:** Mostly tutors, studious college students, and parents with little kids
